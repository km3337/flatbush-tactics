# Flatbush Tactics — Prototype 0.1

Godot 4 starter prototype for the 2.5D rail-shooter / RPG / roguelike concept.

## Implemented
- Banners (`assets/ui/banners/`, drawn by Kristian in `spritework/UI/`, shown at 2x centred): BATTLE START plays for 1.2s when a fight opens (no enemies, shooting or reloading until it's gone); ENEMY DELETED replaces the old CLEARED text when a fight is won. To update one, re-export it from `spritework/UI/` and copy it over the file in `assets/ui/banners/`.
- Text uses the Nokia Cellphone FC pixel font (`assets/fonts/nokiafc22.ttf`), set as the project's default theme font with antialiasing off. It's drawn on an 8px grid, so only use font sizes that are multiples of 8.
- Title screen (`scenes/TitleScreen.tscn`, the first screen): digital rain falls behind everything (`scripts/ui/digital_rain.gd`, `FTDigitalRain`): columns of blocky 5x7 pixel glyphs (generated in code, drawn at 2x) with a near-white head and a trail stepping down through the Lux2k greens, or blues for about 1 column in 6 (`blue_share`, `density`, `speed_range`, `trail_range`, `flicker_rate`). A strip of background colour sits behind PRESS ANY KEY so the rain never runs through it. Menu music: "Secret Portraits" by New Balance (`assets/music/menu_secret_portraits.mp3`) starts 6.05s in, skipping the slow intro, so the logo flies in on the hit at 6.1s (`MENU_SONG_START`), and loops through the title screen and Freelancer Select without restarting (the `Music` autoload, `scripts/music.gd`) and fades out over 1.5s when a freelancer is chosen. On the darkest Lux2k colour, FLATBUSH flies in from the left and TACTICS from the right 0.15s later (0.45s each, overshooting slightly before settling; `fly_seconds` / `tactics_delay`), then PRESS ANY KEY blinks (0.5s on / 0.5s off). Any key, mouse button, tap or gamepad button goes straight to Freelancer Select, even mid fly-in (only presses in the first 0.3s are ignored); it's read in _input so nothing else can swallow it. The logo (`assets/ui/title/logo_flatbush.png` and `logo_tactics.png`, both 358px wide so they line up when landed) is `spritework/logo.aseprite` cropped to the lettering (leaving out the stray dot at the bottom-left of the canvas) and scaled to 0.73x with nearest-neighbour, because the drawn logo is 491px wide; a version redrawn at 360px wide or less can replace it at 1x.
- Freelancer Select (scenes/FreelancerSelect.tscn, after the title screen): built from the layers of `spritework/p1.aseprite`, exported to `assets/ui/freelancer_select/` and `assets/freelancers/ali_portrait.png`. The chevrons (or Left/Right, A/D, swipe) page through the roster; locked slots show a large ?. Below the portrait: name, weapon, bio, starting gear, stats, starting rounds, and CHOOSE. Freelancers are `FTFreelancer` resources (`freelancers/ali.tres`): Ali, Vakabon System, starting gear Pilon Cannon (each hit has a 15% chance to explode for 1 damage to enemies within 80px). The roster and number of locked slots are set on the scene's root node.
- Click/tap anywhere to fire. Shots have a cooldown (`fire_cooldown` on the freelancer: Ali 0.4s; gear can change it through the `fire_cooldown` stat). A tap during the cooldown is buffered: only the latest one is kept, and it fires the moment the cooldown ends. Ducking into cover cancels it.
- The cursor is a reticle on every screen (`Reticle` autoload, `scripts/reticle.gd`), drawn in game pixels; the system cursor is hidden over the window. During the shot cooldown a red bar wraps the bottom of the ring and drains. On touch, the reticle sits where you last tapped. It's drawn in code as a placeholder.
- Space (desktop) / swipe down (touch) ducks into cover and reloads.
- While in cover, a blue placeholder rectangle covers the bottom 65% of the screen (`coverage` on the Cover node). The top 35% stays visible but slightly blurred (`blur_radius`, in game pixels; `shaders/cover_blur.gdshader`), so far enemies show through as soft shapes. The blur fades in and out with the cover slide. Shooting is disabled; you're locked in cover for the reload, then press Space / swipe down again to leave.
- The biome (`biomes/*.tres`, `FTBiome` resource) changes the background and the cover's look: colour and name label (Street → Parked Car, Subway Platform → Support Pillar, Subway Car → Seat Row). Press B to cycle biomes while testing.
- Battle backgrounds (`assets/backgrounds/`): Kristian's photos (`spritework/backgrounds/photos/`) pixelated by `spritework/tools/pixelate_backgrounds.gd`: a tall slice is averaged down to 200x420, auto-levelled, saturated, darkened and snapped to the Lux2k palette (`spritework/lux2k.pal`, no dithering), then drawn at 2x centred, with the palette's darkest colour behind. Each background is an `FTBackdrop` (`backdrops/*.tres`): the texture plus `ground_slots`, the same slot names as the director but placed so feet land on that photo's floor. Walking enemies use those; enemies with `floats` on their type (the Heavy / tentacle brain) use the director's mid-air slots. Enemies are layered by depth (lower feet draw in front), and a shot hits the enemy drawn on top. Each biome lists its backdrops and a battle picks one at random; for the demo every biome has all three (City Hall station, graffiti wall, graffiti corridor). To add a photo, drop it in the photos folder, add a line to `JOBS` in the script, run `Godot --headless --path prototype --script <abs path to the script>`, make a backdrop `.tres` for it with floor positions, and add it to the biomes.
- Open battle-mechanic ideas are tracked in `BATTLE_NOTES.md`.
- Encounters are data. `FTEncounterDirector` (on the `Enemies` node) runs an `FTEncounter` (`encounters/*.tres`): a list of `FTWave`s, each a set of `FTSpawn`s (enemy type + named slot). Wave 1 starts immediately; later waves start when the previous wave is cleared, when at most N enemies are alive, or N seconds after the previous wave. A spawn whose slot is taken waits for it to free up. Slots (`far_left`, `far_center`, `far_right`, `near_center`, `near_right`) are set on the director. When every wave is in and every enemy is dead, CLEARED shows and the battle restarts (prototype). Tick `endless` on the director to loop the encounter instead.
- Enemy stats live in `FTEnemyType` resources (`enemies/*.tres`): Grunt (3 HP, staggerable), Heavy (4 HP, not staggerable, dark red), Runner (2 HP, staggerable, strafes, pink). Dead enemies fade out and are removed. The current wave shows top-right next to the biome.
- Gear (passive run items, like Slay the Spire relics). `FTGear` (`scripts/gear.gd`) defines the hooks (stat changes, shot damage, damage taken, battle start, counter, kill); each gear is a `.tres` in `gear/`, either an `FTStatGear` (pure stat change, no code) or a small script in `scripts/gear/`. `gear/gear_pool.tres` lists every gear; each gear's `sources` flags say whether it can come from the start of a run, elite fights or events. The `Run` autoload (`scripts/run.gd`) holds the owned gear (copies, so gear can keep per-run state), rolls rewards weighted by rarity (60/30/10, no duplicates, seeded per run), and passes hooks to every gear. Owned gear shows as icons under the portrait, with the border colour showing rarity: grey for common, blue for uncommon, gold for rare. Hover for a tooltip, click/tap for the description, and an icon pulses when its gear triggers. New gear pops up its name and description.
- Run stand-in until the route map exists: a run starts on launch with one random starting gear. Battles alternate Test Street → Test Elite. Clearing an elite shows CLEARED, then a reward picker with 3 different unowned elite gear (`Run.roll_gear_choices`); click/tap a card or press 1/2/3 to take one. Cards ignore input for the first 0.4s so a late shot can't pick one, and they never take keyboard focus, so Space can't pick either. Starting and event gear are still single grants. Going down ends the run and all gear is lost. G grants a gear as an event would (debug).
- Starter gear: Speed Loader (reload 30% faster), Extended Mag (+2 rounds), Puffer Jacket (+1 max HP) — common, any source. Brass Knuckles (counters +1 damage; uncommon, elite/event), Bodega Cat Charm (first hit each battle does nothing; uncommon, event only), Last Round (last round in the mag deals double; rare, elite only), Walkman (every 3rd counter heals 1; rare, elite/event).
- New enemies (art cut from `spritework/rips/` by `spritework/tools/cut_enemies2.gd`; credits in `assets/enemies/PLACEHOLDER_CREDITS.md`). The droid's lob uses the blue disc from its sheet (`lob_sheet`), scaled to the projectile's size:
  - **Metal Snake** (`enemies/metal_snake.tres`, walker, 3 HP, staggerable): `burrows`. It rises out of the floor, fires once, sinks, stays underground (can't be hit) for 1.2s, and rises again in a random free slot (the director moves it).
  - **Seeker** (`enemies/seeker.tres`, floater, 2 HP, staggerable): swings slowly along a U-shaped arc around the middle of the screen (`arc_size` / `arc_speed`) and blinks yellow during its wind-up (`windup_tint` + `windup_flash`). Normal shot; cover blocks it.
  - **Dark Droid** (boss, `enemies/dark_droid.tres`, 30 HP): floats on an arc and charges a huge green lob for 2.5s (`lob_charge`, `lob_size`). 3 hits on the droid or the orb during the charge cancel it (`counter_hits`); otherwise it's thrown, can't be shot down (`lob_shootable_in_flight` off) and lands for 3 even behind cover. From half HP it keeps 2 Seekers alive, replacing each 4s after it dies (`summon_*`). Its fight (`encounters/dark_droid.tres`, tier BOSS) is the Last Stop.
  - `encounters/test_tunnel.tres` (Snake + Seeker, then Grunt + Snake + Seeker) is in the stage's battle pool next to Test Street.
- Sound: the BATTLE START banner plays `assets/sfx/battle_start.mp3` (a woosh from Kristian's downloads). Each shot plays the freelancer's `fire_sound`; Ali's (`assets/sfx/ali_shot.tres`) picks one of three laser zaps at random with ±5% pitch. The zaps (`ali_shot_1..3.wav`) are sliced from a Freesound laser clip (`spritework/sfx/laser_14792_freesound.mp3`, which is three zaps followed by 11s of silence). Up to 4 shots can sound at once; dry fire is silent. Menu confirms (pressing a key on the title screen, the checkmark that starts the run, BOARD on Line Select) play `assets/sfx/menu_select.wav`, the effect at 0:10 (9.96s to 10.95s) of a Mega Man Zero SFX collection (`spritework/sfx/mega_man_zero_sfx_collection.mp3`), through the `Music` autoload so it isn't cut off by the scene change.
- Music: battles loop "Go Crazy" by Sam Waitin (`assets/music/battle_go_crazy.mp3`, at -6 dB, `music_volume_db` on the battle scene), starting with the BATTLE START banner. When the fight is won or the player goes down it fades out over 3s (`music_fade_seconds`). Elite fights and the Last Stop (tier ELITE or BOSS) play "Synkro" by Qteku instead (`assets/music/elite_synkro.mp3`), with the same fade.
- The test encounter (`encounters/test_street.tres`): Grunt + Runner → when 1 is left, Heavy + Grunt → 10s later, Heavy + Runner + Grunt.
- Enemies turn orange to telegraph an attack, then fire. Out of cover you take a hit (red flash); in cover it's blocked. Hit/blocked counts are top-right.
- Player HP: Kristian's HP bar (`assets/ui/hud/hp_bar.png`, from `spritework/UI/hp bar.png`) next to Ali's portrait (`assets/ui/hud/portrait_ali.png`, a strip of three 64x64 frames exported from `spritework/UI/player_hurt_RECOVERED.aseprite` without the `_reference` and `_guides` layers: frame 1 idle, 2 hurt, 3 happy; `PORTRAIT_STATES` in `hud.gd`). The bar's 17 pips always show HP as a share of max HP (17 x HP / max, rounded); lost pips are painted with the bar's background green from the right, flashing white for 0.25s (`scripts/ui/hp_bar.gd`). The portrait has three looks: IDLE, HURT (2 HP or less, or downed) and HAPPY (briefly, after a counter or a kill). At 0 HP, DOWN shows and the run ends.
- Counter: shooting an enemy while it's orange shows COUNTER on the left. Staggerable enemies (far left, centre, right strafer) also get their attack cancelled and are stunned (grey, with ★ stars orbiting their head) for 1.5s.
- While in cover, a pulsing orange "!" is drawn on the cover over each enemy winding up an attack; it flashes red when the shot is blocked.
- Portrait mode. The base resolution is 360x780 (9:19.5, modern phone shape), the minimum area every screen shows; other aspect ratios show extra space around it. Freelancer Select is laid out in 360x780; the battlefield and the other screens are still authored in a 360x640 area centred inside it. Pixel-art setup: textures use Nearest filtering; the game scales to fit the window exactly (`canvas_items` + `expand` + fractional scale mode), so at non-whole scales some art pixels are 1px bigger than others. Leftover space shows as extra battlefield area. The battlefield stays centred and the HUD sticks to the screen edges. On desktop the window opens as large as the monitor allows in the game's portrait shape (filling the usable height, title bar included; about 1.27x on a 1080p screen), centred, and can be resized. If the monitor changes shape (e.g. rotated to portrait) or the window moves to another monitor, it re-fits automatically (`scripts/window_fitter.gd` autoload). See `assets/README.md` for adding Aseprite art.
- Freelancer (`freelancers/first.tres`, `FTFreelancer`): 5 HP, 5-round magazine, 0.65s reload, arsenal capacity 20, starting arsenal 12 Plain + 1 Freeze. Gear modifies these (stats `magazine_size`, `reload_seconds`, `max_health`, `arsenal_capacity`).
- Arsenal (deck of rounds). `FTWeapon` shuffles the run's arsenal into a draw pile at battle start; firing spends the chambered round into the discard pile; reloading keeps unfired rounds and tops the magazine up to 5 from the draw pile, reshuffling the discard pile in when the draw pile runs out. Shuffles use the run's seeded RNG. The ammo row colours each loaded round by its modifier (rightmost = next to fire); the line under it shows rounds left in the draw pile and spent.
- Ammo modifiers (`ammo/*.tres`, `FTAmmoMod`; scripts in `scripts/ammo/`), numbers tunable in each .tres. Statuses show as coloured tags under the enemy.
  - Burn: 1 damage every 1.5s, twice; re-hitting restarts it.
  - Toxic: +1 stack; your next shot on that enemy deals +1 damage per stack and clears them (burn/thunder damage doesn't spend stacks).
  - Freeze: 3s; the enemy's wind-up runs at half speed and it stops swaying.
  - Thunder (uncommon): the hit arcs 1 damage to the nearest other enemy.
- Ammo reward after every fight (after the elite gear pick on elites): choose 1 of 3 modifiers from `ammo/ammo_pool.tres` (rarity-weighted), or Skip (S). The round is added to the arsenal; if that puts it over capacity, pick a kind of round to remove one of (cards show counts, no skip). Kills from any source (shots, burn, thunder) trigger gear `on_kill`. The arsenal is lost when the run ends; `Run.start_run` has a marked hook for rounds that carry over later.
- Five-round visual ammo row along the bottom.
- Each spent round jerks/shakes, drops, and fades individually.
- RELOAD: Kristian's sprite (`assets/ui/hud/reload.png`, exported from `spritework/UI/Reload.aseprite`) appears when the magazine is empty. It stays solid, then flashes slowly (1s per flash) if the player hasn't reloaded after 1s (`reload_nag_delay` / `reload_flash_seconds` on the HUD). Reloading hides it.
- Temporary tap/click hit marker.
- Input, weapon state, and HUD are separated so rail movement and RPG systems can be added independently.
