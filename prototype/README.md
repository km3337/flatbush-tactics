# Flatbush Tactics — Prototype 0.1

Godot 4 starter prototype for the 2.5D rail-shooter / RPG / roguelike concept.

## Implemented
- Freelancer Select (scenes/FreelancerSelect.tscn, the first screen): built from the layers of `spritework/p1.aseprite`, exported to `assets/ui/freelancer_select/` and `assets/freelancers/ali_portrait.png`. The chevrons (or Left/Right, A/D, swipe) page through the roster; locked slots show a large ?. Below the portrait: name, weapon, bio, starting gear, stats, starting rounds, and CHOOSE. Freelancers are `FTFreelancer` resources (`freelancers/ali.tres`): Ali, Vakabon System, starting gear Pilon Cannon (each hit has a 15% chance to explode for 1 damage to enemies within 80px). The roster and number of locked slots are set on the scene's root node.
- Click/tap anywhere to fire. Shots have a cooldown (`fire_cooldown` on the freelancer: Ali 0.4s; gear can change it through the `fire_cooldown` stat). A tap during the cooldown is buffered: only the latest one is kept, and it fires the moment the cooldown ends. Ducking into cover cancels it.
- The cursor is a reticle on every screen (`Reticle` autoload, `scripts/reticle.gd`), drawn in game pixels; the system cursor is hidden over the window. During the shot cooldown a red bar wraps the bottom of the ring and drains. On touch, the reticle sits where you last tapped. It's drawn in code as a placeholder.
- Space (desktop) / swipe down (touch) ducks into cover and reloads.
- While in cover, a blue placeholder rectangle covers the bottom 65% of the screen (`coverage` on the Cover node). The top 35% stays visible but slightly blurred (`blur_radius`, in game pixels; `shaders/cover_blur.gdshader`), so far enemies show through as soft shapes. The blur fades in and out with the cover slide. Shooting is disabled; you're locked in cover for the reload, then press Space / swipe down again to leave.
- The biome (`biomes/*.tres`, `FTBiome` resource) only changes the cover's look: colour and name label (Street → Parked Car, Subway Platform → Support Pillar, Subway Car → Seat Row). Press B to cycle biomes while testing.
- Open battle-mechanic ideas are tracked in `BATTLE_NOTES.md`.
- Encounters are data. `FTEncounterDirector` (on the `Enemies` node) runs an `FTEncounter` (`encounters/*.tres`): a list of `FTWave`s, each a set of `FTSpawn`s (enemy type + named slot). Wave 1 starts immediately; later waves start when the previous wave is cleared, when at most N enemies are alive, or N seconds after the previous wave. A spawn whose slot is taken waits for it to free up. Slots (`far_left`, `far_center`, `far_right`, `near_center`, `near_right`) are set on the director. When every wave is in and every enemy is dead, CLEARED shows and the battle restarts (prototype). Tick `endless` on the director to loop the encounter instead.
- Enemy stats live in `FTEnemyType` resources (`enemies/*.tres`): Grunt (3 HP, staggerable), Heavy (4 HP, not staggerable, dark red), Runner (2 HP, staggerable, strafes, pink). Dead enemies fade out and are removed. The current wave shows top-right next to the biome.
- Gear (passive run items, like Slay the Spire relics). `FTGear` (`scripts/gear.gd`) defines the hooks (stat changes, shot damage, damage taken, battle start, counter, kill); each gear is a `.tres` in `gear/`, either an `FTStatGear` (pure stat change, no code) or a small script in `scripts/gear/`. `gear/gear_pool.tres` lists every gear; each gear's `sources` flags say whether it can come from the start of a run, elite fights or events. The `Run` autoload (`scripts/run.gd`) holds the owned gear (copies, so gear can keep per-run state), rolls rewards weighted by rarity (60/30/10, no duplicates, seeded per run), and passes hooks to every gear. Owned gear shows as icons under the portrait, with the border colour showing rarity: grey for common, blue for uncommon, gold for rare. Hover for a tooltip, click/tap for the description, and an icon pulses when its gear triggers. New gear pops up its name and description.
- Run stand-in until the route map exists: a run starts on launch with one random starting gear. Battles alternate Test Street → Test Elite. Clearing an elite shows CLEARED, then a reward picker with 3 different unowned elite gear (`Run.roll_gear_choices`); click/tap a card or press 1/2/3 to take one. Cards ignore input for the first 0.4s so a late shot can't pick one, and they never take keyboard focus, so Space can't pick either. Starting and event gear are still single grants. Going down ends the run and all gear is lost. G grants a gear as an event would (debug).
- Starter gear: Speed Loader (reload 30% faster), Extended Mag (+2 rounds), Puffer Jacket (+1 max HP) — common, any source. Brass Knuckles (counters +1 damage; uncommon, elite/event), Bodega Cat Charm (first hit each battle does nothing; uncommon, event only), Last Round (last round in the mag deals double; rare, elite only), Walkman (every 3rd counter heals 1; rare, elite/event).
- The test encounter (`encounters/test_street.tres`): Grunt + Runner → when 1 is left, Heavy + Grunt → 10s later, Heavy + Runner + Grunt.
- Enemies turn orange to telegraph an attack, then fire. Out of cover you take a hit (red flash); in cover it's blocked. Hit/blocked counts are top-right.
- Player HP: 5 pips top-left next to a placeholder portrait (coloured box + word). The portrait reacts to hitting (NICE), missing (MISSED), countering (YEAH!), killing (GOT 'EM) and getting hit (OUCH), and shows HURTING at 2 HP or less. At 0 HP, DOWN shows and the battle restarts after 1.5s.
- Counter: shooting an enemy while it's orange shows COUNTER on the left. Staggerable enemies (far left, centre, right strafer) also get their attack cancelled and are stunned (grey, with ★ stars orbiting their head) for 1.5s.
- While in cover, a pulsing orange "!" is drawn on the cover over each enemy winding up an attack; it flashes red when the shot is blocked.
- Portrait mode. The base resolution is 360x780 (9:19.5, modern phone shape), the minimum area every screen shows; other aspect ratios show extra space around it. Freelancer Select is laid out in 360x780; the battlefield and the other screens are still authored in a 360x640 area centred inside it. Pixel-art setup: textures use Nearest filtering and the game scales by whole numbers only (`canvas_items` + `expand` + integer scale mode), so pixels stay square; leftover space shows as extra battlefield area. The battlefield stays centred and the HUD sticks to the screen edges. On desktop the window opens at the largest whole-number multiple of 360x640 that fits the monitor (centred), and can be resized. If the monitor changes shape (e.g. rotated to portrait) or the window moves to another monitor, it re-fits automatically (`scripts/window_fitter.gd` autoload). See `assets/README.md` for adding Aseprite art.
- Freelancer (`freelancers/first.tres`, `FTFreelancer`): 5 HP, 5-round magazine, 0.65s reload, arsenal capacity 20, starting arsenal 12 Plain + 1 Freeze. Gear modifies these (stats `magazine_size`, `reload_seconds`, `max_health`, `arsenal_capacity`).
- Arsenal (deck of rounds). `FTWeapon` shuffles the run's arsenal into a draw pile at battle start; firing spends the chambered round into the discard pile; reloading keeps unfired rounds and tops the magazine up to 5 from the draw pile, reshuffling the discard pile in when the draw pile runs out. Shuffles use the run's seeded RNG. The ammo row colours each loaded round by its modifier (rightmost = next to fire); the line under it shows rounds left in the draw pile and spent.
- Ammo modifiers (`ammo/*.tres`, `FTAmmoMod`; scripts in `scripts/ammo/`), numbers tunable in each .tres. Statuses show as coloured tags under the enemy.
  - Burn: 1 damage every 1.5s, twice; re-hitting restarts it.
  - Toxic: +1 stack; your next shot on that enemy deals +1 damage per stack and clears them (burn/thunder damage doesn't spend stacks).
  - Freeze: 3s; the enemy's wind-up runs at half speed and it stops swaying.
  - Thunder (uncommon): the hit arcs 1 damage to the nearest other enemy.
- Ammo reward after every fight (after the elite gear pick on elites): choose 1 of 3 modifiers from `ammo/ammo_pool.tres` (rarity-weighted), or Skip (S). The round is added to the arsenal; if that puts it over capacity, pick a kind of round to remove one of (cards show counts, no skip). Kills from any source (shots, burn, thunder) trigger gear `on_kill`. The arsenal is lost when the run ends; `Run.start_run` has a marked hook for rounds that carry over later.
- Five-round visual ammo row along the bottom.
- Each spent round jerks/shakes, drops, and fades individually.
- RELOAD flashes when the magazine reaches zero.
- Temporary tap/click hit marker.
- Input, weapon state, and HUD are separated so rail movement and RPG systems can be added independently.
